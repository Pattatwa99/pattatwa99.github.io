Minecraft is, most definitely, one of the formative games of my life. Its extraordinary approach to player freedom and elegantly simple design make it a joy. The virtues of Minecraft are such that it is worthy of extollation, highest praise, and deepest revererance. From its Java based, run everywhere ethos, to the consumer friendly practices it takes with modding and self hosting, playing Minecraft is a uniquely rewarding experience.
As of late, me and some chums have been playing a server together. One of my friends has a VPS he hosts on, and a small core play pretty regurally. We have cleared the game, and continue to play as a group on a weekly or so basis. A small series of structures dots the ridge on which we have our bases. It grows a bit every day. Me and my bestest friend have assembled a small series of structures, and our current work is House-House.
House-House is rather cool. It's a cheery, poplar, and pale oak mansion. I'm very pleased with its progress. It's two stories, and has a large copper wall in front, much to the annoyance of my fellow builder. It is, in my opinion, a good House-House. I should like to live in such a structure someday.
